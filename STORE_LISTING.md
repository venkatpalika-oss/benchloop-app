## Full description (Google Play max 4000 chars; App Store max 4000)
Bench & Loop puts the calculations chemists, lab technicians and instrumentation engineers do every day into one fast, offline app. Every result updates as you type, shows the formula used, and flags values that need attention.

LAB CALCULATORS
• Molarity and mass to weigh — leave any one field empty and the app solves for it
• Dilution C1V1 = C2V2 — with diluent volume and dilution factor
• Concentration converter — %, g/L, ppm, ppb, mol/L, mmol/L
• pH and pOH — from pH, [H+], [OH-], strong acids and strong bases
• Buffer pH (Henderson–Hasselbalch) — plus the ratio needed for a target pH
• Beer–Lambert law — absorbance, ε, path length or concentration, with %T
• Standard curve — paste your standards to get slope, intercept, R², a chart and the unknown's concentration
• Centrifuge RCF ↔ RPM

INSTRUMENTATION CALCULATORS
• 4–20 mA scaling — mA, process value and % span, with a 5-point table and NAMUR NE 43 fault flags
• Five-point calibration check — as-found errors in % of span with pass/fail against your tolerance
• RTD Pt100 / Pt500 / Pt1000 — temperature ↔ resistance (IEC 60751, Callendar–Van Dusen)
• Type K thermocouple — mV ↔ °C with cold-junction compensation (NIST ITS-90)
• DP flow — square-root extraction for orifice and venturi meters
• DP level — liquid height from differential pressure and specific gravity
• Pressure unit converter — bar, psi, kPa, MPa, kg/cm², mmHg, inH2O and more
• Control valve Cv / Kv sizing for liquids

DESIGNED FOR THE JOB
• Works completely offline — in the lab, the plant or the field
• Favourites and recent calculators for one-tap access
• Your inputs are remembered between sessions
• Copy any result to paste into a report or logbook
• Light and dark themes
• No ads, no accounts, no data collection

Results are provided for engineering guidance. Always verify critical values against your site procedures and calibrated references.
