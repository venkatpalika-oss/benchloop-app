Bench & Loop Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

Bench & Loop is a laboratory and instrumentation calculator app for Android and iOS. This policy explains what the app does with information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and makes no network requests.

Information stored on your device

The values you enter, your favourite calculators, your recently used calculators and your theme choice are saved in the app's local storage on your device so they are available next time. This data never leaves your device. You can erase it with Settings → Reset all calculators, or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

The app requests no device permissions such as camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files.

Children

The app is a technical tool intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
